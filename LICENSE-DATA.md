## What this covers

- `data/brands.json` and the files generated from it: `public/brands.json`, `public/brands.csv`,
  `public/brand-directory.js`, and `docs/BRANDS.md`
- `data/pe-firms.json` and the files generated from it: `public/pe-firms.json`, `public/pe-holdings.csv`,
  `public/pe-firm-directory.js`, and `docs/PRIVATE_EQUITY_FIRMS.md`
- the kosher certification listings in `public/kosher-places.js`
- the research notes in `docs/`

These records summarize and link to public sources such as SEC filings, company announcements, and news
reports. The license covers the compilation and the written notes; the linked sources belong to their
publishers.

## What it does not cover

- **Place data** from Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap, which the app loads at runtime. See
  [Overture attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) and the
  [OpenStreetMap copyright page](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- **Google Maps content**, which is shown under Google's terms when a person connects their own key and is
  never stored in this repository.
- **Third-party libraries** in `public/vendor/`, which keep their own licenses.

## No warranty

Ownership changes often. The data is researched but incomplete, may be out of date, and is provided as is.
Check the linked source before relying on any record.
