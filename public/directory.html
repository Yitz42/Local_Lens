What the labels cover

Corporate brand includes any company with equity listed on a stock exchange, regardless of size, its owned brands, and large privately held chains. Private equity backed means documented investment by a private equity firm; some entries describe minority stakes. Publicly traded companies with documented PE backing appear in both categories. Either filter will hide them. A listed investor alone does not make a private portfolio company publicly traded.

A brand and its local operator can have different owners. These entries identify brand affiliation, not verified ownership of every franchise or fuel station. Businesses missing from this list remain unknown in the app.

To suggest an addition or correction, open a GitHub issue with the brand name and a current supporting source.
