A CLEARER VIEW

Behind every place,
there’s a bigger story.

Local Lens helps you explore places with a little more context. Our ownership labels come from a reviewed directory of company, investor, and regulatory sources. Browse and download the full brand list.

Independent / co-op A source identifies an independently owned business or a member-owned cooperative.

Corporate brand Any company with equity listed on a stock exchange, plus its owned brands and large privately held chains. Public companies with documented PE backing qualify for both filters.

Private equity backed A brand appears in a private equity firm’s current investment portfolio. This may include minority investments.

Unknown ownership We haven’t matched the place to a verified source. Unknown does not mean independent.

The brand and the local owner can differ.

Filters apply to brand affiliation, not verified ownership of an individual location. Franchises and branded gas stations may be owned by local operators. Open a place to see the scope and supporting source.

Hide names your way.

Name filters can hide known excluded brand names inside a longer listing, such as 7-Eleven Fuel. Add your own names to hide any matching listing, regardless of ownership. Matches use complete words and ignore case and punctuation. Your choices stay on this browser and apply to the map, list, and saved places. Name matches control visibility without changing verified ownership labels.

Choose a food type.

Italian, pizza, and other food types use the place data’s own tags. Checking several types includes places matching any checked type. Some places have no food-type data; their names and brands are not used to guess a cuisine.

A growing directory, with honest gaps.

The directory currently focuses on selected US brands. It is not a complete or automatically updated ownership database. Each brand entry shows its own source review date; ownership can change.

Published places. Your private choices.

Locations come from the September 23, 2026 Overture Maps release, read from published map tiles through our server. This is a dated dataset, not real-time business verification. Completeness and operating status may vary. Recent area results are saved on this device. During an outage we may show results up to seven days old, with a visible notice. Distances are straight-line estimates. Search uses Nominatim only when you submit. Approximate coordinates are sent to our server to select nearby place-data tiles. Map and location-search providers receive the requests needed to display and search the area. Recent area results, saved places, and filter choices are stored in this browser; recent area results expire after seven days.
