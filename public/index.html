Local Lens helps you explore places with a little more context. Our ownership labels come from a reviewed directory of company, investor, and regulatory sources. Browse and download the full brand list.

Independent / co-op A source identifies an independently owned business or a member-owned cooperative.

Corporate brand A major national or multinational company, including large privately held chains.

Private equity backed A brand appears in a private equity firm’s current investment portfolio. This may include minority investments.

Unknown ownership We haven’t matched the place to a verified source. Unknown does not mean independent.

The brand and the local owner can differ.

Filters apply to brand affiliation, not verified ownership of an individual location. Franchises and branded gas stations may be owned by local operators. Open a place to see the scope and supporting source.
