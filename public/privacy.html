Privacy

Updated October 25, 2026.

Local Lens stores filter choices and saved places in your browser. When Google Maps is connected, its browser API key is stored on this browser and sent directly to Google. Google place names, addresses, coordinates, and other listing content are kept in memory for the current visit; only Google place IDs are saved for bookmarks. Saved Google places are fetched again when you open Saved. Disconnecting removes the key from this browser; bookmarks remain until removed.

Google receives the map, search-center coordinates, and Places requests needed to provide listings. Location permission is requested only when you choose “Use my location.” Google services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

When Google is disconnected, Local Lens uses published Overture data and OpenStreetMap tiles. Nearby Overture records may stay on this device for up to seven days. Submitted city or address searches go to Nominatim. Existing non-Google saved places remain on the device until removed. These providers and the hosting platform process the requests needed to deliver their services.

Local Lens application code does not log API keys or location coordinates, and does not send browsing activity to an advertising or analytics service. Hosting and map providers may keep their own operational logs. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes its local preferences, connection, and bookmarks.
