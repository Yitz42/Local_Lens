# US grocery and restaurant chain review

Reviewed October 25–26, 2026, using the supplied [supermarket chain list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_supermarket_chains_in_the_United_States) and [restaurant chain list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_restaurant_chains_in_the_United_States) to discover gaps. Company, investor, and regulatory sources support each published classification. Wikipedia is a discovery reference, not ownership evidence.

This pass adds **164 brands: 116 groceries and 48 restaurants, cafes, and dessert concepts**. The additions comprise 159 corporate/large-chain brands and five PE-backed grocery brands. After that portion of the review, the directory contained **525 distinct corporate or PE brands**, with overlapping filter memberships of 395 corporate and 143 PE-backed brands. The subsequent [gas, coffee, and retail expansion](GAS_AND_RETAIL_REVIEW.md) records the current totals. Two local independent reference records are separate from these totals. The searchable directory, map matcher, CSV, JSON, and [full source-linked report](BRANDS.md) use the same canonical data.

## Regional names and matching

`matchBounds` is an optional array of geographic rectangles in canonical data and the JSON/CSV downloads. Both exact ownership matching and automatic brand-name exclusion honor these guards, even when place data supplies an explicit brand field. Reused aliases are valid only when every scope is disjoint. Invalid or overlapping bounds fail the directory build.

Examples include Food City (Bashas’ in Arizona versus K-VA-T in the Southeast), northeastern Price Chopper versus the separate Kansas City network, Kroger’s City Market versus similarly named Wisconsin stores, northeastern Foodtown versus Houston Food Town, and Brookshire’s Super 1 versus Miners’ Super One. The rectangles are conservative service-area guards, not state polygons, store geofences, or proof of a location’s owner. They may leave valid stores outside a conservative boundary unmatched.

Hawaii’s researched Times and related banners now fall within the general coverage guard. Alaska, Puerto Rico, and the US Virgin Islands are also included in that guard, without claiming comprehensive brand research there. Locations outside the broad researched US coverage remain unknown. Personal hidden-name choices remain global and independent of the ownership directory.

## Remaining review limits

This is an expanded selected directory, not a claim that every name on either Wikipedia page is current or covered. The lists include closed businesses, renamed banners, small independent operators, cooperative networks, and names with multiple unrelated owners.

Earth Fare and FreshDirect were left without new ownership rules because current ultimate ownership was unresolved in the primary evidence reviewed. Historical Ahold or PE relationships were not reused. Ambiguous HFC and supplier-customer relationships were not assigned an owner. Closed or converted grocery banners were not bulk-added as active stores. Taco Time and other restaurant names requiring a further regional or current-parent check were deferred. A missing match means **unknown**, never independent.

Brand affiliation does not establish whether an individual map listing is current, open, independently operated, or owned by the parent. Live places and ownership research remain separate data layers.
