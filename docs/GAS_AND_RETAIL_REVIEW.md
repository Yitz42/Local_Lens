## Fuel and convenience additions

The 53 additions include Valero, Sunoco, Phillips 66, Conoco, 76, CITGO, Gulf, Sinclair, Murphy USA, Murphy Express, QuickChek, Casey’s, CEFCO, Kwik Trip, Kwik Star, Maverik, Kum & Go, RaceTrac, RaceWay, Pilot, Flying J, One9, Love’s, Stewart’s Shops, Buc-ee’s, Cenex, Cenex Zip Trip, DK Fuel, Alon, Cumberland/EG banners, GPM/ARKO banners, United Pacific/Rocket, Jacksons, and ExtraMile. QuickChek was consolidated into one record despite appearing in two research batches.

Fuel branding, ownership of a station's real estate, ownership of the convenience-store business, and the local operating company are distinct. A branded station can be independently operated. Notes preserve that distinction, and independently owned customers are not assigned wholesale-supplier ownership merely for buying fuel.

### PE and regional groups

[Cumberland Farms Limited's September 2026 F-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2123232/000119312526382176/d113224df1a.htm) establishes TDR backing through EG Group / Optima and identifies eleven US banners: Cumberland Farms, Turkey Hill Minit Markets, Loaf N’ Jug, Certified Oil, Tom Thumb convenience stores, Kwik Shop, Minit Mart, Quik Stop, Fastrac, Coen Markets, and Sprint Food Stores. Cumberland Farms Limited is the renamed issuer; EG Group Limited remains its direct parent. The filing confirms Coen's June 1 closing. An IPO registration is not a completed listing, and a reported takeover proposal is not a completed sale. The TDR catalog entry records this selected holding with partial-review scope.

[GPM's current brand family](https://www.gpminvestments.com/) and [ARKO's corporate information](https://www.arkocorp.com/company-information) support Admiral, BreadBox, E-Z Mart, Fas Mart, Li’l Cricket, Roadrunner Markets, Scotchman, Shore Stop, and Village Pantry. The owner listing is ARKO Corp.; it is not substituted with a separate wholesale subsidiary's listing.

[Fortress's current PE portfolio](https://www.fortress.com/what-we-do/private-equity/middle-market-private-equity) supports United Pacific and Rocket stores. Roadrunner Markets is a separate GPM banner. A station using a fuel brand also sold by United Pacific is not thereby Fortress-backed. Jacksons and ExtraMile are separate reviewed records, with ExtraMile's joint-venture and franchise scope stated explicitly.

## Matching and remaining scope

Texas grocery Tom Thumb and southeastern convenience Tom Thumb share a name but have different owners. Disjoint geographic guards now apply in exact ownership and automatic name exclusions. Regional guards also protect Admiral, Jacksons, and Sprint. Short ambiguous names such as 76, Gulf, Pilot, Rocket, and DK are category-restricted; this can intentionally leave convenience-only records unmatched when provider categories are insufficient. PUMA retail is restricted to the other-store category to avoid the unrelated fuel brand. Kwik Trip, Kwik Star, Kwik Shop, Quik Stop, QuikTrip, and QuickChek remain distinct brands.

These are broad service-area and category safeguards, not store-level ownership verification. Unmatched places remain unknown. Personal hidden names still apply globally. Historical or converted brands do not establish that a listed store is currently operating.

All app categories have received additional reviewed coverage, but the directory remains selected and manually reviewed. Royal Farms, Rutter's, Blue Bottle's pending ownership transition, and other unresolved candidates were not assigned guessed owners. Canadian/Mexican ownership, every local franchise entity, and an exhaustive inventory of all corporate or PE companies remain outside this review.
