### Exclusions and historical traps

- **Benihana:** The ONE Group’s August 2026 results confirm current corporate parent and restaurant operation: https://ir.togrp.com/press-releases/detail/564/the-one-group-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results . However, calling it simply “no PE backing” would be premature: the completed 2024 acquisition used $160 million of preferred equity primarily issued to Hill Path: https://ir.togrp.com/press-releases/detail/530/the-one-group-reports-second-quarter-2024-financial-results . The 2024 agreement specifies $150 million Hill Path and $10 million HPS preferred allocations: https://ir.togrp.com/all-sec-filings/content/0001558370-24-003954/stks-20240326xex10d2.htm . An opened October 2, 2026 Form 4 shows continuing Hill Path-related reporting/board share grants but **does not itself prove the exact continuing preferred stake**: https://ir.togrp.com/all-sec-filings/content/0001493152-26-045529/ownership.html?TB_iframe=true&height=auto&preload=false&width=auto . Excluded from additions pending a current preferred-equity review.
- **Ariat:** Wikipedia category is historical; LNK’s 2012 announcement describes a sale to Fisher family and management: https://www.lnkpartners.com/ariat-international-announces-private-sale-agreement/ . Current official Ariat About page does not resolve ownership. Excluded rather than assign old LNK/Brentwood sponsor ownership or pretend this establishes a 2026 stake. Source: https://www.ariat.com/about-us .
- **Au Bon Pain:** Opened article says Ampex acquired the brand in 2021, after LNK / Panera. The bounded pass did not find sufficiently current primary ownership evidence. No Panera/JAB/LNK label imported from category membership. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Au_Bon_Pain .
- **Checkers / Rally’s:** Current article and discovery results indicate lender restructuring after Oak Hill ownership; lender equity and local franchise transfers must be distinguished. No current primary equity schedule was established in this bounded review. Excluded rather than apply old Oak Hill affiliation. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checkers_and_Rally%27s .
- **99 Cents Only Stores, Abraham & Straus, Anchor Blue, Barneys, and other defunct/rebranded category members** were not treated as active current storefront chains merely because a Wikipedia page exists.
- Already-listed brands, including Albertsons, At Home, Birkenstock, Bob Evans, California Pizza Kitchen, Church’s, Cinnabon, CKE banners and other existing A–F records, were not duplicated or relabeled solely from category membership.

All records identify chain affiliation or sponsor backing. They do not prove the equity owner of an individual franchise, dealership, licensed location, or shopping center tenant. No unrelated sister brand is used as an alias.
