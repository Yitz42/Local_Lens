# PESP employer tracker review

Reviewed October 17, 2026. Research starting point: [Private Equity Stakeholder Project’s employer tracker](https://pestakeholder.org/explore-the-issues/labor-and-jobs/largest-private-equity-employer-database/).

This pass reviews the page’s public company examples and follows their current investor and company sources to related retail, restaurant, and pharmacy brands. It is a selected review, not a full import of the embedded Airtable database. The page displays a November 2025 update near the top and a December 2024 database date near the bottom; neither date is treated as a current ownership guarantee. Every added record has its own October 17, 2026 source review date.
